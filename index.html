Built by a sports parent

It started with one athlete’s journey.

SportsJurni began with a simple problem: one dad wanted a better way to preserve his daughter’s hockey journey.

The first lessons. The early practices. The tournaments. The teammates. The big saves. The milestones.

The photos existed—but the story was scattered everywhere.

After hearing the same frustration from parents across many sports, that personal project became SportsJurni: one place for every sport, every season, and every memory.

Your memories are yours. Your family’s sports memories should stay in your control.
Parent-controlled. You choose what to save, share, export, print, or delete.
Clear support. Privacy, terms, account help, and support are always easy to find.
